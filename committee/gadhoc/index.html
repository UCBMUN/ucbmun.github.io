Meet the Dais


Sara Khemani

Chair

My name is Sara Khemani and I am so excited to serve as your chair for the GADHOC. UCBMUN was one of the first clubs I joined in college and has been an extremely big part of my college life and I am so excited to share that with all of you! I have been involved with Model UN since the 8th grade and I compete on the collegiate circuit as a GA del.

I was born in Bombay, India and spent most of my life growing up in Singapore and now go to UC Berkeley, where I am on the pre-PHD track majoring in Molecular and Cell Biology and Political Economy. My academic interests lie at the intersection of global health, research, and policy and I hope to work in pharma in either immunology or oncology in the future. I also do research at a lab in Berkeley and UCSF and am involved with Berkeley’s Healthcare Business Association and Project RISHI. Outside of school, I love binge watching TV shows (Modern Family and Gossip Girl are my favorites!), getting matcha or poke with my friends, and going on walks or hikes around the Berkeley hills. I also absolutely love going to the beach and going into SF to the marina and also shop.

I'm so excited to meet you all this March!

Best,
Sara Khemani
Chair, General Assembly Ad-Hoc of the Director General
