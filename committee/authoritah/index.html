About the Committee

Welcome to the most unhinged PTA meeting this side of the Rockies!

Here at South Park Elementary, a minor scheduling mishap or an expired cafeteria meatloaf instantly escalates into a full-scale town panic. After continuous teacher strikes, the legendary cough medicine ban, and whatever happened during the last holiday pageant, the school board wants this Parent Teacher Association permanently disbanded.

As avid members of the South Park Parents Facebook group and the broader community, delegates must determine what the future of the Parent Teacher Association will look like, if there is to be a future at all. Whether dealing with sudden bans on Terrance and Phillip, mysterious supernatural anomalies ruining the science fair, or Mr. Garrison launching a propaganda campaign before lunch, survival means balancing the wants of the commmunity with the needs of the students, building alliances with other outrage-obsessed parents, and above all else, ensuring the blame lands on someone else. Whatever happens in this room today, remember the core tenet of this community: respect our authority, or watch this entire town burn to the ground.
