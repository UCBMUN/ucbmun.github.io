About the Committee

"I’ve got a text!" Welcome Islanders to paradise. Set against the backdrop of the Villa, the JCC Love Island committee splits delegates into two factions: the Villa and Casa Amor. While Casa Amor plots to shake up the status quo, the main Villa must balance public optics, brand deals, producer interference, and deep-seated betrayals to maintain total control of the island. This isn't just about finding "the one", but it's a high-stakes power struggle where recoupling ceremonies can change the game and islander drama can spark full-blown chaos. As Villa members, you will navigate secret alliances, producer-manufactured twists, Casa Amor infiltrations, and public votes that can upend your carefully built empire in an instant. Will you stick with your day-one alliance, or will your head be turned by a new bombshell entering the villa? We are looking for the baddest, the bold, the amazing who can blend strategic thinking with camp reality TV energy. Bring your boldest selves, your sharpest wit, and your best game plan because in this Villa, loyalty is tested, drama is currency, and only the most ruthless strategists survive.


Meet the Dais


Victoria Gutanu

Chair

Hi! I’m Victoria Gutanu, a junior studying Political Science and Economics, and I am very happy to serve as a Chair for the JCC Love Island All Stars (The Villa) committee! I am interested in international relations, and more specifically, Eastern European politics. Besides Model UN, I write for the World section of the Berkeley Political Review, do research for the Institute of European Studies and the Berkeley Center on Comparative Equality and Anti-Discrimination Law, am a part of Romanians at Berkeley, and play tennis. I first joined UCMUN my freshman year, with my first MUN experience ever being staffing The Sicilian Mafia Committee at UCBMUN XXIX. Since then, I have competed at a few collegiate conferences, met some fantastic delegates on the circuit, and am now Director General for our high school conference, BearMUN. While competing, I have especially enjoyed the Press Corps committees as I can go and observe multiple other committees in action. However, what I am most excited to see in action are you Islanders in drama-filled challenges this weekend! I urge you to take a creative approach, and hope you are looking forward to surviving the villa--I know I am!

Best,
Victoria Gutanu
