About the Committee

As short form content becomes the dominant source of entertainment across the globe, television networks are facing a troubling question: how can they produce content that retains a viewer’s attention? In this committee, delegates will take on the roles of network television screenwriters, turning events happening across committees into potential storylines for hit shows and posing their observations as their own pitches. From accidental leaks that force last minute rewrites to terrible ratings that threaten cancellation, delegates will experience the thrills and challenges that come with being a screenwriter. Do you have what it takes to write the next hit show? Or will your pitch force the network to pull the plug?

Dear Delegates,

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the Press Corps committee at UCBMUN! My name is Romi Vieira Lisboa (she/her), and I will serve as your director for this committee!

I’m a fourth-year student at UC Berkeley studying English and Creative Writing. I joined UCBMUN my freshman year to explore new activities at Cal and fell in love with staffing and competing in Press Corps ever since. Outside of MUN, I love to write personal essays and commentaries, particularly pieces about theater!

The Press Corps committee is one that encourages a lot of innovative and creative work from its delegates. Although television writing already requires much creativity, this weekend we hope to open your minds to the ways that creative writing and reporting can merge.

If there are any questions or concerns please do not hesitate to contact us. I’m so excited to meet you all and witness the amazing pieces you produce during the weekend!

Best,
Romi Lisboa
