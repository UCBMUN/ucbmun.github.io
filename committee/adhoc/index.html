About the Committee

The Adhoc of the Secretary General is a committee that is confidential until shortly before the conference begins. By the time committee opens, delegates will have enough information to understand the world they are entering but not enough to know exactly what challenges await.

Rishit Pradhan

Crisis Director

My name is Rishit Pradhan and I am a Senior (oh no) studying Chemical Biology and concentrating in Computational Chemistry. I am from the incredible town of Bakersfield in the beautiful central valley of California. When I am not buried in Chem homework or doing work for this committee, I am usually elbow-deep in neurosurgery research out at UCSF, or trying to ski. This past summer I also had the chance to work with folks out in Silicon Valley, which was a completely different kind of chaos than the OR, but one I loved all the same. Outside of that, I am probably playing tennis or watching whatever sport is on (I have been glued to the US Open lately).

I thought of this committee because I have always had a soft spot for mystery, the kind where you don't know what's coming until it's already happened, and an ad hoc has been on my bucket list since I started doing MUN. There is something irresistible about walking into a room with no topic, no backstory, and no idea what's about to be thrown at you. I am confident that this committee will be very unnatural and unique, and everyone will walk out of here having explored crisis in a way that has never been seen before.

I wanted something that feels just a little off-balance, where alliances twist faster than you can track them and everyone second-guesses who they trust. Whether you love solving puzzles, weaving rumors, or just throwing yourself into chaos for the fun of it, I hope you find something here that surprises you. I cannot wait to see the stories you all create once the gates close and the unknown finally reveals itself.

Best,
Rishit Pradhan
