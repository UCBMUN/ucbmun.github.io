About the Committee

The year is a few clicks past now, fandom as you know is drowning. Publishing houses have quietly slipped generative AI models into their recent releases and slowly AI tools have colonized every fic archive. Readers can no longer tell if the update they just devoured took a human twelve hours, or a machine twelve seconds. You are modern day literatures’ last hope. You, the writers, who’ve built this world word by word, the ones whose chapters inspired thousands of tropes, whose slow-burns broke comment sections, whose fandoms would riot would you vanish. Now, you’re being asked to hand over your pen over to something that has read everything you’ve ever written, and can now write it back to you faster, cheaper, and tirelessly. This committee focuses on obsession, passion, authorship, and the strange communities built within comment sections and Discord servers. Do you organize? Do you adapt? Do you burn it all down, or die trying? Delegates will write as the established fanfic authors they worked so hard to become. Bring your keyboards, your fandoms, your ships, your beef, and decide whether individually or together, if the future of fanfiction still has room for the humans that built it.


Meet the Dais


Sophia Martinez

Chair

Hello Delegates,

My name is Sophia Martinez and it is my pleasure to welcome you all into this vast and exciting side of the internet. Before AI slop and brainrot, teens rotted their brains primarily with fanfiction, varying from niche fandoms, to even IRL-ships. Through this passion for different shows, games, and other media, grew a devoted following towards fanfiction and their writers. From 300k word Naruto fanfictions, to 1,000 word Yuri on Ice fanfictions, people throughout the 2010’s indulged themselves in the places that these writers immersed themselves. I look forward to what you all create and not generate as the practice of fanfiction writing dwindles against the rise of AI today. I have been involved in UCBMUN since my freshman year, competing every now and then. I never was involved in MUN in High School so the collegiate circuit was my introduction to the world of Model UN! I am originally from Orange County, so meeting people on both the circuit and within UCBMUN is incredibly rewarding with how diverse Model UN is. This will be my fourth UCBMUN and second UCBMUN as a chair. Last year, I chaired the JCC Romeo and Juliet committee and absolutely loved that experience. Being in the frontroom and seeing the heated debates and discussions was incredibly fun to watch, and even better when seeing what the backroom came up with. Outside of Model UN, I am currently studying English and Public Policy, and hope to enter the field of law after my time at Cal. Outside of academics, I am a part of a pre-law fraternity on campus and work as a barista! I am super interested in coffee and a dream of mine after I retire from working in law is opening a cafe and running that place like the navy. Additionally, I enjoy playing tennis, and reading in my spare time (#whatasurprise). I look forward to meeting you all in committee!

Best,
Sophia Martinez
