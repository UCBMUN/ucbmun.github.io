About the Committee

It's late 1976. The biggest musical group at the time, Fleetwood Mac, is in the process of recording what will become one of the greatest albums of the 70s. In the creation of their top-charting album, Rumors, and the planning of their subsequent massive world tour, the band faces many conflicts that threaten to end them. They are rapidly rising towards unprecedented levels of both fame and notoriety as the band members and those invested in the band’s success grapple with the growing strife within the group. Often thought of as one of the most drama-filled bands in recent history, the band's stories have become the inspiration for works of fiction dramatizing the real situations facing this group. From extremely public breakups to financial struggles to music industry pressures, the band seems to be at a breaking point. This committee aims to replicate this era in their history and help guide Fleetwood Mac through the dilemmas that could destroy them. How will they navigate finishing up production on their groundbreaking album amid all of the interpersonal drama brewing? How will the band be able to come together and put on the tour of a lifetime despite the growing pressures on them from fans and executives? How will Fleetwood Mac manage to break the chain of constant turmoil and escape from this experience unscathed? When the rain washes you clean, we’ll know.

In my free time, I love reading (major shoutout to Daisy Jones and the Six, which is particularly relevant to this committee…), and I also love portrait photography and usually have a camera in my hand. And obviously, I LOVE music, especially some good soft rock like Fleetwood Mac. I’m usually listening to one of my many, many playlists on Spotify.

Although I have so much fun competing as part of our travel MUN team, one of my favorite parts of UCBMUN is staffing our conferences, both at the collegiate level and at the high school level. The past two years, I have had the wonderful opportunity to be a USG and then the Secretary-General of UCBMUN, and have realized staffing is definitely my favorite part of MUN. This is why I am so excited to chair this committee and see what you all do and what creative solutions you come up with to the unhinged problems that we throw your way!

Celeste and I cannot wait to see what you all have in store for us! If you have any questions, feel free to email me, and I’d be glad to answer them! audreywatson@berkeley.edu
