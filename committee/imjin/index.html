About the Committee

In the year 1592, the Korean Peninsula finds itself beset by a great power: the Armies of Taik? Toyotomi Hideyoshi. Japan has decided Korea is a nice stepping stone to conquering all of Ming China. Korea's Joseon Kingdom needs help, and Buddhist Monks have taken up arms to defend her. A sect of roughly 5,000 monks, organized by Supreme Commander of the Eight Provinces and Sixteen Schools Hyujeong, have taken up arms and coordinated to defend the homeland. These so called peaceful seekers of enlightenment have united and strapped themselves to the yoke of necessity in order to ward off the invaders. The peninsula awaits with baited breath as the Korean and the Japanese armies collide. Not only is this worldly conflict brewing, you all are soldiers, generals, and kings in a subcutaneous battle between those seeking to escape samsara and the devil, Mara. Across time and space, Mara seeks to trap beings in a cycle of death and rebirth and prevent enlightenment. As incarnations of the Buddhist Pantheon, it is your jobs to defend the Saha and guide the beings within towards enlightenment. This conflict is never-ending and will occur over many different times and places. Are we in the final stages of the Buddhist world, or shall the monks usher in a new age of Nirvana?
