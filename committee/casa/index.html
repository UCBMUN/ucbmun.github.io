About the Committee

As the sun begins to rise over the islands of Fiji, the Villa doors are opening once again. This year's Islanders are no strangers to love or heartbreak but what happens when they allow their walls to come down and escape from the real world. We are leaving behind the casual dating realm of coffee chats and dating apps and entering a world of dramatic fire pit recoupling surprise bombshell arrivals and late night chats in soul ties. For weeks Islanders have explored connections and created unforgettable memories but now everything changes. Welcome to Casa Amor. A place where loyalties are questioned, heads are turned and even the strongest couples may begin to crack. New Islanders, hidden agendas, and irresistible temptations threatened to turn the Villa upside down. Secrets will be exposed, alliances will shift and every text message. . . well lets just say you wont know whats coming next. Perhaps an old flame enters the Villa unexpectedly or maybe we'll stir the pot with a game of truth or dare or a headline challenge with an unexpected dumping. Will you stay loyal to the person waiting for you in the Villa? Or will you risk it all for a connection that could be even stronger? Will you secure your place in the finale, become this season's ultimate villain, or find yourself standing alone at the fire pit? Only time will tell. . . because in Casa Amor nobody is ever as closed off as they claim to be.
